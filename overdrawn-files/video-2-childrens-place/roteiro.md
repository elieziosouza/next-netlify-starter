### 2:40 — De onde veio o problema

[CARTÃO] "THE MALL PROBLEM"
[MOTION] ícones de loja apagando um a um num mapa simples dos EUA

> To understand why, you have to look at where these stores were. The Children's Place grew up in the American shopping mall. And for more than a decade, mall traffic has been falling.
>
> Parents changed how they shop. Kids' clothes are now one click away on Amazon, at Walmart, at Target, at Shein. Those competitors sell at huge scale and can afford to go cheaper. A specialty store that only sells kids' clothes, inside a mall, pays rent and staff every month whether people walk in or not.
